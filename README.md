## About Me

I'm AJ Arish, an engineer, creator, entrepreneur and creative technologist interested in building things at the intersection of technology, business, creativity and human behaviour.

I don't see my career as belonging to a single box.

I enjoy moving between software, artificial intelligence, digital products, business, content, psychology, neuroscience, research and experimentation — understanding how different disciplines connect and turning those connections into useful things.

My approach is simple:

**Learn deeply. Build practically. Think independently. Communicate clearly. Keep evolving.**

I use this GitHub as a long-term record of the things I build, test, study and explore.

Some repositories represent finished work.

Some are experiments.

Some are ideas becoming products.

And some are simply evidence of something I was trying to understand.
